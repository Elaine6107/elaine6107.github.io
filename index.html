Elaine

Ph.D. Student in Computer Science

I am a Ph.D. student at Fudan University, advised by Prof. Bowen Zhou. I am also a research intern at Shanghai Artificial Intelligence Laboratory, where I am supervised by Dr. Tai Wang and Dr. Jiangmiao Pang.

My research interests include spatial intelligence, MLLMs, and agentic RL. My recent work explores spatial understanding and reasoning with vision-language models, agentic RL for spatial reasoning with world models, and physical consistency in visual generation.

Previously, I received my master’s degree in Mechanical Engineering from Peking University and my bachelor’s degree in Robotics Engineering from Harbin Institute of Technology.
